💰 Refund Policy — How Client Can Refund Their Payment

Effective: May 13, 2026 | Paystack Only | Ghana + 8 Countries | Every request saves: Client Photo + Name + Phone + Email + Time + Patient + Nurse + Booking ID + Paystack Ref

✅ QUICK ANSWER: How Client Can Do Refund?

Go to client-dashboard.html → My Bookings (my-bookings.html — Real Date) → Refund Query (refund-query.html) → Select Booking → Fill Reason + Amount + MoMo Number → Submit. CEO sees in ceo-refund-management.html with your photo + phone + email → Approves → Pays via Paystack to your MoMo/Card in 3-5 days.

📝 STEP-BY-STEP — How Client Can Do Refund

1
Open My Bookings
From Client Dashboard, tap My Bookings → points to my-bookings.html (shows real date e.g. MAY 13, not JUL 17). You see all bookings: Accepted, Rejected, Pending, with Nurse Photo + Phone + Patient + Amount + Paystack Ref + Time.
2
Go to Refund Query Form
Tap Refund Query → points to refund-query.html — This is NOT history page, this is WHERE client makes refund request! Page shows your Client Card: Photo + Name + Phone + Email (ASABERE PETER example). Select Booking to Refund from dropdown (loads your bookings with real dates).
3
Fill Refund Request Form
- Refund Reason: Choose: Nurse Rejected / Nurse No-Show / Cancelled Before Accept / Cancelled Within 24h / Double Payment
- Explain Details: E.g. "Nurse Ama rejected LCG123, reason low rate, I want refund to book higher rate"
- Amount: GHS amount (e.g. 150)
- Method: MoMo Ghana (MTN/Vodafone/Airtel) or Card Outside Ghana or Original Paystack Method
- Destination: Your MoMo number (024XXXXXXX) or Card ending
Click Submit Refund Request — With Client Photo Name Phone Email
4
CEO Receives & Processes
Your request saves to Firebase: refundRequests/{id} + allRefundRequests/{id} + bookings/{id}/refundRequested with:
👤 Client: Photo URL + Name + Phone + Email + LatLng + Time (en-GH)
🏥 Patient: Name + Phone + Location + LatLng
👩‍⚕️ Nurse: Name + Phone + Photo + ID
📅 Booking: ID + Service + Amount + Paystack Ref + Status + Time
CEO sees LIVE in ceo-refund-management.html with stats: Pending / Approved / Paid / Rejected / Total GHS → Can Call Client, WhatsApp Client, Open Paystack Dashboard, Approve, Mark as PAID.
5
Get Money Back
After CEO taps Mark as PAID (after Paystack transfer), you see status in refund-query.html history + client-alerts.html. Money returns to your MoMo (Ghana) or Card (outside Ghana) in 3-5 business days. Transaction fees (Paystack 1.9%) may not be refunded.

💯 When You Get 100% Refund

  • Before Nurse Accepts: Cancel before nurse taps Accept ✅ in my-job-offer.html → 100% refund. Use refund-query.html Reason: "Cancel Before Accept"
  • Nurse Rejects: Nurse taps Reject ❌ → You see alert in client-alerts.html with nurse photo phone → 100% refund OR increase rate and book again in book-nurse.html
  • Nurse No-Show: Nurse accepted but didn't come to patient location → 100% refund + file complaint in client-complaint.html (saves your photo name phone email + time + audio/video evidence) → CEO verifies GPS + complaint → 100% refund
  • Double Payment: Paid twice → 100% extra refunded

💛 When You Get 50% Refund

  • After Accept, Within 24 Hours, Service Not Started: Nurse accepted but you cancel within 24 hours of booking time (real date/time saved) → 50% refund. Use Reason: "Cancel Within 24h"

❌ When No Refund

  • After Service Starts: Once nurse arrives at patient location (verified by auto-navigation in my-job-offer.html + GPS) and starts care → No refund. Exception only if nurse no-show.

📱 MoMo Ghana vs Card Outside Ghana

Ghana clients: Refund goes to MoMo number you enter in Destination field (024XXXXXXX). Outside Ghana: Refund to original card. Always include +233 country code for Ghana. Provide correct number — wrong number = delay.

⏰ Timeline

Request via refund-query.html → CEO approves in ceo-refund-management.html (usually within 24h) → CEO transfers via Paystack Dashboard → Mark as PAID → You receive in 3-5 business days. Track in refund-query.html history + WhatsApp CEO button (233544121814).

⚠️ Important: Request Refund BEFORE Delete Account

If you want to delete account, request refund first in refund-query.html, then go to delete-account.html. After deletion, no new refund requests allowed, but pending refunds still processed because your photo name phone email + time already saved in refundRequests.
