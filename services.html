Services Policy - LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING

Professional home nursing care across Ghana and beyond. Verified nurses, hourly pricing, 24/7 support.

1. What We Offer

LOVE CARE GLOBAL provides trained, verified nurses for home-based care. Our services include:

  • General Home Nursing: Daily care, medication administration, vital signs monitoring, wound dressing.
  • Elderly Care: Companionship, mobility support, personal hygiene, chronic disease management.
  • Post-Surgery Care: Recovery monitoring, wound care, physiotherapy assistance, medication compliance.
  • Child & Baby Care: Newborn care, pediatric nursing, immunization support.
  • 24-Hour Live-In Care: Continuous care at client's home - daily, weekly, monthly packages.
  • Hospital Companion: Nurses to stay with patients in hospital.
  • Specialized Care: Diabetes, hypertension, stroke recovery, palliative care.

2. Nurse Verification

All nurses are verified by CEO (lovecareglobal@gmail.com) before they can receive job offers:

  • Photo ID, National ID, Nursing Licence, Certificate, CV, Intro Video
  • GPS location and actual address verified
  • Phone and email verified
  • Status: Pending → CEO Approves → Approved → Can login and receive jobs
  • Blocked nurses cannot receive jobs or chat (CEO can block/unblock at CEO Dashboard)

3. Pricing Policy - Hourly Based

Pricing is calculated as: Hourly Rate × Hours Per Day × Quantity (days/weeks) + Transport

  • Ghana (GHS): Example 50 GHS/hour (CEO sets hourly in CEO Dashboard → Pricing). Daily 8h = 400 GHS, Weekly 40h = 2000 GHS, Monthly 160h = 8000 GHS. 24h live-in: Daily 24h = 1200 GHS.
  • Outside Ghana (USD): Example 5 USD/hour. Daily 8h = 40 USD, etc.
  • Hours From Start/End Time: Client selects Start Time and End Time (e.g., 08:00-17:00 = 9 hrs). System calculates total hours automatically.
  • Client Offer: Client can offer above minimum. Nurse sees offer in My Job Offers.
  • Payment: Paystack (GHS, NGN, ZAR, USD, GBP) - payment before booking is sent to nurse.

CEO sets hourly rate at ceo-dashboard.html → Pricing. All clients see updated rates automatically.

4. Booking & Job Offer Flow

  • Client searches nearby nurses (2km, 5km, 10km, 20km, All Ghana) on map
  • Client selects nurse, fills patient name, phone with country code (+233), service type, Start/End time, quantity
  • System calculates total hours and amount, client pays via Paystack
  • Booking saved to: bookings/{id}, nurseBookings/{nurseId}/{id}, myJobOffers/{nurseId}/{id}, jobOffers/{id}, clientBookings/{phone}/{id}
  • Nurse receives job in My Job Offers page (my-job-offer.html) with Booking ID, client phone (+country code), amount, patient details
  • Nurse accepts job, contacts client, service begins

5. Client Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate patient information, address, GPS, phone with country code
  • Ensure safe working environment for nurse
  • Pay agreed amount before service starts (Paystack)
  • Sign Consent Form (consent-form.html) before care begins
  • For 24h live-in: Provide accommodation, meals, resting area for nurse

6. Nurse Responsibilities

  • Provide professional, compassionate care as per service booked
  • Keep patient information confidential
  • Arrive on time as per Start Time, work until End Time
  • Update CEO or client in case of emergency, use SOS page
  • No extra charges beyond agreed offer unless approved by CEO

7. Safety & Quality

  • All nurses approved by CEO only - no unverified nurse can receive jobs
  • CEO can wipe group chat and block misbehaving nurses at CEO Dashboard → Group Chat Master Controls
  • Nurses group chat (nurse-group-chat.html) - clean chat, no spam, CEO monitored
  • Ratings system: Clients can rate nurses after service (ratings collection)
  • SOS Alerts: For emergencies, use sos.html - CEO and nurses notified

8. Cancellation & Refund

See Refund Policy (refund.html) - cancellations allowed before nurse acceptance. After acceptance, 50% refund if within 24h, no refund after service starts unless nurse fails to appear.
