1. Our Story

LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING was born in Accra, Ghana, from a simple pain - finding a trusted, verified nurse for home care was too hard, too risky, too expensive. Families needed elderly care, post-surgery care, maternity support, but no platform verified nurses properly. CEO decided to build platform that verifies nurses like banks verify customers - strict, manual, 5-step. Today we serve 8 countries.

2. Our Mission

To make trusted home nursing accessible, affordable, and safe for every family worldwide, while empowering professional nurses to earn fairly with dignity. We believe every patient deserves care at home with verified professionals.

3. What We Do - Services

  • Elderly Care: Companionship, medication, mobility, daily living support at home
  • Post-Surgery Care: Wound care, medication, recovery monitoring
  • Maternity & Newborn: Mother and baby care, breastfeeding support
  • Disability Support: Daily assistance, therapy support
  • General Nursing: Injections, vital signs, chronic care
  • SOS Emergency: One-tap emergency alert to contacts + admin + GPS

4. How It Works - Simple 4 Steps

Step 1: Download app, create account
Step 2: Search nurse by country, city, service, price - see verification badge, license, reviews
Step 3: Book date/time, pay via Paystack (secure)
Step 4: Nurse comes to your home, care provided, 85% to nurse automatically

5. Nurse Verification - Our Pride (8 Countries)

We NEVER auto-approve nurses. Every nurse manually verified by CEO:

Ghana: N&MC License + Ghana Card + Certificate + Video
Nigeria: NMCN License + NIN + Certificate + Video
South Africa: SANC License + SA ID + Certificate + Video
Kenya: NCK License + Kenya ID + Certificate + Video
USA: State Board License + US ID + Certificate + Video
UK: NMC UK PIN + UK ID + Certificate + Video
Australia: AHPRA Registration + AU ID + Certificate + Video
Canada: Provincial College + Canada ID + Certificate + Video

6. Payments - PAYSTACK ONLY For All 8 Countries

Why Only Paystack? Simple & Secure!

LOVE CARE GLOBAL business is registered in Ghana. Paystack supports Ghana businesses. Paystack allows INTERNATIONAL CARDS - so USA, UK, Canada, Australia clients CAN pay with their local Visa/Mastercard even though Paystack is African gateway. This solves Stripe complexity!

  • Gateway: Paystack - PCI-DSS Level 1, SSL Encrypted, Licensed
  • Africa Clients: Ghana MoMo (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), Nigeria Bank/USSD, SA EFT, Kenya M-Pesa, plus Visa/Mastercard
  • USA/UK/Canada/Australia Clients: Pay with your local Visa, Mastercard, Amex - Paystack accepts USD, GBP, CAD, AUD international cards and converts to GHS settlement
  • Split: 85% Nurse, 15% Platform - automatic via Paystack Split
  • Security: We NEVER store card details - all handled by Paystack secure servers
  • Currencies: GHS, NGN, ZAR, KES, USD, GBP, CAD, AUD via international card

7. Safety & SOS

Safety is #1. All bookings tracked, SOS button sends GPS + alert to emergency contacts + admin. SOS is extra safety only - always call official emergency first: Ghana 112, Nigeria 112/199, SA 10111/10177, Kenya 1199, USA/Canada 911, UK 999/112, Australia 000.
