Who We Are

LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING is a premium digital healthcare platform established with a single mission: to make professional, compassionate, and affordable home nursing accessible to everyone, everywhere. We bridge the gap between patients in need of home care and highly qualified, verified, and experienced nurses.

Founded in Ghana, we have expanded our operations to 8 countries including Ghana, Nigeria, United States of America, United Kingdom, Canada, Germany, South Africa, and the United Arab Emirates (UAE). Our platform is built on trust, professionalism, and love for humanity.

Our Mission

To provide 24/7 reliable, professional, and compassionate home nursing services that allow patients to heal, age, and receive care in the comfort and dignity of their own homes, surrounded by loved ones.

Our Vision

To become Africa's largest and the world's most trusted home nursing network, setting the global standard for home healthcare delivery.

Our 13 Core Nursing Services

  • 1. Elderly Care: Compassionate support for seniors with daily activities, companionship, and health monitoring.
  • 2. Post-Surgery Care: Professional wound dressing, medication management, and recovery monitoring after surgery.
  • 3. Baby and Mother Care: Antenatal, postnatal care for mothers and newborn baby care by specialized nurses.
  • 4. Disability Support: Daily living assistance and specialized care for persons with disabilities.
  • 5. Chronic Disease Management: Long-term care for diabetes, hypertension, stroke patients.
  • 6. Wound Care: Professional dressing and infection prevention.
  • 7. Medication Administration: Timely and accurate medication giving and monitoring.
  • 8. Physiotherapy Support: Basic mobility and rehabilitation support at home.
  • 9. Palliative Care: Dignified and compassionate end-of-life care.
  • 10. Injection and IV Services: Safe administration at home.
  • 11. Vital Signs Monitoring: Regular BP, sugar, temperature checks.
  • 12. Hospital to Home Transition: Seamless care after discharge.
  • 13. Emergency Home Nursing: Rapid response for urgent care needs.

Why Choose Us?

All our nurses are verified with Nursing and Midwifery Council certificates, background checked, and rated by previous patients. Our app features real-time booking, secure payments, GPS tracking of nurse arrival, and 24/7 customer support. We are available on Android, iOS, and Web as a PWA.
