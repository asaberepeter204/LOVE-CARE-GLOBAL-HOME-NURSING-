📋 Bookings Assigned to You — Accept / Reject

After client pays with Paystack (Full or 50%), booking is saved to nurseBookings/{yourNurseId}/{bookingId} and bookings/{bookingId} and jobOffers/{bookingId}. You will see it here with client details, hours, amount, Google Maps link. You can Accept (start job) or Reject (free yourself).

Loading your job offers from Firebase...
💰 How it works:
1. Client books you → Selects you on map → Watches your video preview → Taps Paystack Full — GHS 244 — Tap to Pay (or 50% button) → Paystack prompt opens → Pays
2. Your code saves to 3 places:
  • bookings/{bookingId} = All bookings
  • nurseBookings/{nurseId}/{bookingId} = Your personal bookings (this page reads from here)
  • jobOffers/{bookingId} = Job offers pool
3. You open My Job Offers → See client name, phone, patient, hours, total, transport, Google Maps location, amount paid (Full or 50%), Paystack ref, 80/20 split
4. You tap ✅ Accept → Status = accepted → Client notified → You start job
  You tap ❌ Reject → Status = rejected → You are freed for other bookings, client can book another nurse
5. CEO dashboard also sees all bookings
