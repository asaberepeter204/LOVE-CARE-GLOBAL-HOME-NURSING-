1. Who we are

LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING provides licensed nurses to your doorstep in 8 countries (Ghana, Nigeria, UK, USA, Canada, Germany, South Africa, UAE). We operate at https://asaberepeter204.github.io/LOVE-CARE-GLOBAL-HOME-NURSING-/ and as an installed PWA / Android APK built via PWABuilder.

2. Information we collect

  • Client data: Name, phone, location (for GPS verified visit), address, care type, payment info via Paystack (we do not store card numbers).
  • Nurse data (2-step verification): Photo, government ID, nursing licence, video intro, GPS location at registration, phone, bank details for payouts.
  • Booking data: Booking ID, date/time, service type, ratings, chat messages in nurse-group-chat.
  • Technical data: IP, device type, browser, PWA install status, approximate location (for live tracking).
  • SOS data: Location and phone when you use SOS Emergency.

3. How we use it

  • Match clients with verified nurses, show photo/video/rating before booking.
  • Live GPS tracking of nurse to client (with consent).
  • Process payments (Paystack GHS/USD + 50% online option, Pay on Delivery).
  • Verify nurses (CEO manual check of ID, licence, video).
  • Safety, fraud prevention, no double-booking (availability lock).
  • Support via WhatsApp +233544121814 and Call +233544121814.
  • Improve app, offline mode, push notifications for bookings.

4. Sharing

We share only what is needed:

  • Clients see nurse profile (photo, first name, rating, video intro) — not ID or bank.
  • Nurses see client booking address/phone for that job only.
  • Paystack for payments, Firebase (if enabled) for bookings, GitHub Pages for hosting.
  • We do NOT sell data. We do NOT share with advertisers.
  • Law enforcement if required by law in Ghana or service countries.
