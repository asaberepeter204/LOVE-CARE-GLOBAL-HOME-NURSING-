At LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING, we respect your right to privacy and to delete your account at any time. This page explains exactly how to request deletion, what data will be deleted, and what data we may retain for legal reasons as required by Google Play Store.

Option 1: Delete Directly From App (Recommended)

Steps:

  1. Open LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING App
  2. Go to Profile > Settings > Account
  3. Click "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm with your password/OTP
  5. Your account will be scheduled for deletion immediately

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to lovecareglobal@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: DELETE MY ACCOUNT - LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING
  • Body: Your full name, phone number registered, email registered, and reason (optional)
  • We will verify your identity via OTP and delete within 48 hours

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Your profile: name, phone, email, photo, address
  • Your booking history and preferences
  • Your login credentials and authentication tokens
  • Your chat messages with support (after 30 days)
  • Your location history

What Data May Be Retained?

For legal, tax, and medical record compliance in Ghana and other countries, we may retain:

  • Transaction records for 5 years (as required by Ghana Revenue Authority) - anonymized
  • Anonymized booking records for analytics - no personal identifiers
  • Any data required by law enforcement if there is an ongoing investigation

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose all booking history, nurse connections, and remaining wallet balance. If you have an ongoing booking, please complete or cancel it first before requesting deletion.
