User Consent and Permissions

App: LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING

Website: https://asaberepeter204.github.io/LOVE-CARE-GLOBAL-HOME-NURSING-/

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

By downloading and using LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING, you provide explicit consent for the data collection and usage described below, in compliance with Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843) and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Consent to Data Collection

You consent to collection of personal information (name, phone, email, location), health-related information necessary for care (condition, medications, allergies), device information, and usage data. Health information is considered sensitive and is only collected with your explicit checkbox consent during booking.

2. Consent to Location

Our app requires location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to: show nearby nurses, calculate distance and cost, allow nurse to navigate to your home, and for safety tracking during service. Location is collected only when you request a service or when a service is ongoing. You can deny location but you must manually enter address, which may delay service.

3. Consent to Communications

You consent to receive SMS, email, WhatsApp, and push notifications regarding booking confirmations, nurse arrival, OTP codes, payment receipts, and service updates. You can disable marketing notifications in settings but transactional notifications cannot be disabled for safety.

4. Consent to Data Sharing with Nurses

You consent that when you book a service, your name, phone, address, and care requirements will be shared with the assigned verified nurse solely to deliver the service. Nurses are bound by confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from sharing your data.

5. Consent to Photos and Records

With your permission, nurses may take photos of wounds (with consent) for medical documentation. These are stored securely and shared only with you and authorized medical personnel you approve.

6. Withdrawal of Consent

You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account via app or emailing lovecareglobal@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. If you withdraw consent for essential data (like location or phone), we may not be able to provide services.

7. Age Consent

By using this app, you confirm you are 18+ or have parent/guardian consent. For patients under 18 or elderly without capacity, consent must be provided by legal guardian.
