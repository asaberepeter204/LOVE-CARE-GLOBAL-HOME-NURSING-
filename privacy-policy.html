Privacy Policy for LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING

App Name: LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING

Website: https://asaberepeter204.github.io/LOVE-CARE-GLOBAL-HOME-NURSING-/

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Contact: lovecareglobal@gmail.com

At LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING, operated from Accra, Ghana, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains in detail what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and your rights regarding your data. By using our mobile application and website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information: When you register, we collect your full name, phone number, email address, home address/GPS location, profile photo, age, gender. For nurses, we also collect nursing council ID, certificates, years of experience, and qualifications.

B. Health Information: To provide appropriate care, we may collect information about the patient's condition, medications, allergies, medical history, and care requirements. This is only collected with explicit consent.

C. Usage and Technical Data: We collect device information, IP address, app usage logs, booking history, payment transaction IDs (we do not store card numbers), and location data when you request a nurse.

D. Communications: When you contact support, we keep records of your messages and calls for quality assurance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match patients with the nearest and most qualified available nurses
  • To process bookings, calculate costs, and process secure payments via Paystack/Flutterwave
  • To send booking confirmations, nurse arrival alerts, and service updates via SMS, email, push notification
  • To verify nurse credentials and ensure patient safety
  • To improve our app, analyze trends, and develop new services
  • To comply with legal obligations in Ghana and other operating countries

3. How We Share Your Information

We share only necessary information: Patient name, phone, address, and care needs are shared with the assigned nurse only after booking confirmation. Nurse name, photo, qualifications, and ratings are shared with patient. We use Firebase (Google) for secure storage. We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties for advertising. We may share data if required by law enforcement with valid court order.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, Firebase Authentication, encrypted databases, and restricted access. Only authorized personnel can access sensitive data. Despite best efforts, no system is 100% secure, but we continuously monitor for threats.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. Booking history is kept for 5 years for medical and legal records. If you request account deletion, we delete personal data within 48 hours, except data we must keep for legal compliance.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information at any time from app settings or by emailing lovecareglobal@gmail.com. You can also withdraw consent or disable location, though it may affect service delivery.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Bookings for children must be made by a parent or legal guardian.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you via app notification and update the Last Updated date at the top. Continued use after changes means acceptance.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at lovecareglobal@gmail.com or visit https://asaberepeter204.github.io/LOVE-CARE-GLOBAL-HOME-NURSING-/contact.html
