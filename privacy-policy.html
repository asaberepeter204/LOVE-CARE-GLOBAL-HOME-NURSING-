1. Introduction

LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING ("We", "Us", "Platform") operates in Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, USA, UK, Australia, and Canada ("Operating Countries"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal data when you use our app and website. By using our services, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information You Provide:
  • Name, phone number, email address
  • Profile photo, gender, date of birth
  • Government ID (for nurse verification)
  • Nursing certificate, license number (e.g. Ghana N&MC, Nigeria NMCN, South Africa SANC, Kenya NCK, USA State Board, UK NMC, Australia AHPRA, Canada Provincial College)
  • Address, location data (for nurse matching)
  • Booking details, health needs (limited to care required)
B. Automatically Collected:
  • Device type, OS, app version, IP address
  • Usage data: bookings, clicks, time spent
  • Location: only when you grant permission, for nearby nurse search and SOS feature
C. Payment:
  • We use Paystack (Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya) and Stripe (USA, UK, Australia, Canada). We do NOT store your card number, CVV or bank details. Handled securely by Paystack/Stripe.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To verify nurses (ID, certificate, video verification)
  • To match clients with nearby verified nurses
  • To process bookings and payments (15% platform, 85% nurse)
  • To send booking confirmations, updates, SOS alerts
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve app security and prevent fraud/spam (3-second chat limit)
  • To comply with laws in Operating Countries

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on your Operating Country:

  • Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya: Consent, contract performance, legal obligation (NDPA Nigeria, Data Protection Act Ghana, POPIA South Africa, Data Protection Act Kenya 2019)
  • USA: Consent and contract, following HIPAA principles for health information handling
  • UK: Consent, contract, legitimate interest (UK GDPR)
  • Australia: Consent and contract (Privacy Act 1988, Australian Privacy Principles)
  • Canada: Consent and contract (PIPEDA)

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We NEVER sell your personal data. We share only when necessary:
  • With Nurses/Clients: Name, location, booking details for service fulfillment
  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for hosting/database, Paystack/Stripe for payments, Google Maps for location (if enabled)
  • Legal Requirement: If required by law in any Operating Country, court order, or to protect safety
  • Business Transfer: If company is merged or acquired, users will be notified

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Data stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
  • Access restricted to authorized personnel only (CEO, verified staff)
  • Nurse IDs and certificates stored securely, accessed only for verification
  • We implement reasonable security measures, but no system is 100% secure. Please protect your login
  • Data may be stored/processed in US/EU servers via Google Firebase, with safeguards for international transfers

7. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while account active
  • Deleted accounts: Data deleted within 30 days except legal retention
  • Booking/payment records: Retained up to 7 years as required by financial laws in Operating Countries (e.g. tax laws in USA, UK, Australia, Canada, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya)
  • Nurse verification records: Retained as required by nursing council regulations in respective Operating Country

8. Your Rights

Depending on your Operating Country, you have right to:

  • Access your data (request copy)
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (App > Settings > Delete Account or Delete Account Page)
  • Withdraw consent (may affect ability to use app)
  • Object to processing (where applicable under UK GDPR / POPIA / PIPEDA)
  • Lodge complaint with data authority in your Operating Country (e.g. UK ICO, Australia OAIC, Canada OPC, Kenya ODPC, Nigeria NDPC, Ghana DPC)

To exercise rights: Email lovecareglobal@gmail.com - we respond within 30 days.

9. Location Data & SOS

Location collected only if you allow. Used for:

  • Finding nearby verified nurses
  • SOS emergency alert to nearby nurses

You can disable location in phone settings anytime. SOS is extra safety only, NOT replacement for 112 / 911 / 999 / 000. Always call official emergency services first in your Operating Country.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is for users 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 (or under 13 in USA/Canada). If we learn we have, we will delete immediately.

11. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies for functionality and analytics. App does not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in browser settings.

12. Third-Party Links

App may contain links to Paystack, Stripe, Firebase. Their privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for third-party practices.

13. International Data Transfers

Because we operate in 8 countries (Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, USA, UK, Australia, Canada), your data may be transferred across borders via Firebase/Google servers. We ensure appropriate safeguards per UK GDPR, Australia Privacy Act cross-border rules, PIPEDA, POPIA, NDPA requirements.
